Beyond The Chart

A Ten-Year Comparative and Predictive Analysis of Amazon, Apple, Google, Microsoft, Accenture and
Nvidia Stocks Using Python.
Overview
This project analyzes stock market performance from 2016-2025 using:
. Python
· Pandas
. NumPy
. Matplotlib
· Seaborn
. SciPy
Objectives

. Data Cleaning
. Growth Analysis
· Risk Analysis
. Correlation Analysis
· Beta Analysis
. Monte Carlo Simulation
. Predictive Modeling
Companies Studied

· Amazon
. Apple
· Google
. Microsoft
. Accenture
· Nvidia
Key Results
. Nvidia showed highest overall growth.
. Microsoft and Apple performed best among conventional stocks.
. Monte Carlo projections were found optimistic during backtesting.
